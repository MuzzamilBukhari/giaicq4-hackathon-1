# Module 4: Vision-Language-Action & Humanoid Robotics

## Overview

Welcome to Module 4 of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics curriculum. This module focuses on the integration of multimodal AI systems with humanoid robotics, covering the essential concepts that enable robots to perceive, understand, and act in the physical world through vision-language-action (VLA) systems.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems in robotics
- Apply humanoid kinematics principles including forward and inverse kinematics
- Explain bipedal locomotion concepts including ZMP theory and MPC control
- Integrate VLA systems with ROS, simulation environments, and deployment platforms
- Design safe and effective humanoid robot behaviors

## Prerequisites

Before starting this module, you should have:

- Completed Modules 1-3 of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics curriculum
- Basic understanding of ROS 2 concepts and message types
- Familiarity with simulation environments (Gazebo, Isaac Sim, or Unity)
- Basic knowledge of linear algebra and calculus for kinematics

## Module Structure

This module is divided into four comprehensive chapters:

### Chapter 1: VLA Fundamentals
Explore the core concepts of vision-language-action systems that enable robots to perceive, understand, and execute complex tasks through multimodal AI.

### Chapter 2: Humanoid Kinematics
Learn the mathematical foundations of humanoid movement, including forward kinematics, inverse kinematics, and joint-space transformations.

### Chapter 3: Bipedal Locomotion
Understand the principles of stable walking and balance control in humanoid robots, including ZMP theory and gait generation.

### Chapter 4: Integration & Deployment
Combine all concepts into a complete pipeline from high-level instructions to robot behavior, with considerations for real-world deployment.

## Integration with Previous Modules

This module builds upon the foundations established in Modules 1-3:

- **Module 1**: ROS 2 fundamentals and communication patterns
- **Module 2**: Simulation environments and digital twin concepts
- **Module 3**: NVIDIA Isaac Sim and perception pipelines

The content in this module demonstrates how these components work together in the context of humanoid robotics and VLA systems.
