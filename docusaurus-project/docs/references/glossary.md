# Glossary

A comprehensive reference of key terms and concepts used throughout the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics textbook.

---

## A

**Action** - A ROS 2 communication pattern for long-running, goal-oriented tasks with feedback and cancellation support. See also: Service, Topic.

**AGX Orin** - High-end NVIDIA Jetson platform for AI and robotics applications, featuring powerful GPU and CPU cores.

**AI Agent** - An autonomous software system that perceives its environment and takes actions to achieve specific goals.

---

## C

**colcon** - Command-line tool for building ROS 2 workspaces, replacing catkin from ROS 1.

---

## D

**DDS (Data Distribution Service)** - Middleware standard used by ROS 2 for peer-to-peer communication without a central master.

**Digital Twin** - A virtual replica of a physical robot or system used for simulation, testing, and validation.

---

## G

**Gazebo** - Open-source 3D robot simulator with physics engine, commonly used with ROS.

---

## I

**IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit)** - Sensor that measures acceleration, angular velocity, and sometimes magnetic field.

**Isaac Sim** - NVIDIA's GPU-accelerated robotics simulation platform built on Omniverse.

---

## J

**Jetson** - NVIDIA's embedded computing platform for AI and robotics at the edge.

---

## K

**Kinematics** - Study of motion without considering forces; essential for robot arm control.

---

## L

**LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)** - Sensor using laser pulses to measure distances and create 3D maps.

---

## M

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)** - Protocol for integrating AI models with applications and data sources.

**Message** - Structured data type used for communication in ROS 2 (topics, services, actions).

---

## N

**Nav2** - ROS 2 navigation framework for autonomous mobile robots.

---

## O

**Omniverse** - NVIDIA's platform for 3D simulation and collaboration, foundation for Isaac Sim.

---

## P

**Publisher** - ROS 2 node that sends messages to a topic.

---

## Q

**QoS (Quality of Service)** - Policies in ROS 2 controlling reliability, durability, and latency of communications.

---

## R

**rclpy** - ROS 2 Client Library for Python, used to create ROS 2 nodes in Python.

**ROS 2** - Robot Operating System 2, middleware framework for building distributed robotic applications.

**RViz** - 3D visualization tool for ROS, used to display sensor data, robot models, and more.

---

## S

**Service** - ROS 2 communication pattern for synchronous request-response interactions.

**SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping)** - Technique for building a map while tracking robot position.

**Subscriber** - ROS 2 node that receives messages from a topic.

---

## T

**Topic** - Named channel in ROS 2 for publish-subscribe communication.

---

## U

**Unity** - Real-time 3D development platform, used for robot visualization and simulation.

**URDF (Unified Robot Description Format)** - XML format for describing robot kinematics and dynamics.

---

## V

**VLA (Vision-Language-Action)** - Multimodal AI models that integrate vision, language, and action for robotic tasks.

---

## X

**Xacro** - XML macro language for creating modular, reusable URDF files.

---

## Z

**ZMP (Zero Moment Point)** - Concept in bipedal robotics for maintaining balance during locomotion.
