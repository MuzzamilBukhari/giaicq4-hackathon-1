# Research Findings for Module 1 — ROS 2 Fundamentals

## Phase 0: Outline & Research

### NEEDS CLARIFICATION: ROS 2 Distro Selection

**Research Question**: Which specific ROS 2 distro (Humble or Iron) should be used as the primary target for all code samples, testing, and documentation?

**Options Considered**:
- **ROS 2 Humble**: Long Term Support (LTS) release, stable, widely adopted, good community support. Compatible with Gazebo Classic.
- **ROS 2 Iron**: Newer release, potentially includes more recent features/improvements, but shorter support window. Potentially better compatibility with Gazebo Garden/Ignition.

**Rationale**: A decision needs to be made to ensure consistency across all examples, labs, and documentation. The choice impacts available packages, recommended simulation environments, and overall stability for the target audience.

**Decision**: ROS 2 Humble
